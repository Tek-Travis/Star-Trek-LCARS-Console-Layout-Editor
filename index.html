Guide, provenance, and limitations

Select a part on the board or in the layer list. Drag to move; use its yellow corner to resize. Text and colors update immediately. Turn Edit mode off to test joysticks and sliders. Scanner labels support {x} and {y}.

Canvas resizing changes the workspace only; existing elements are not automatically rescaled or deleted. Rotated parts may extend beyond the canvas and be clipped. No autosave: export before closing.
